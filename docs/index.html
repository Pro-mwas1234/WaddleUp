Quick Setup

Install on Android in 30 Seconds

No Play Store wait needed — grab the signed APK straight from your phone browser.

1

Tap Download Latest APK

Tap the orange download button above on your Android phone to save the newest .apk installer directly.

2

Open & Allow Once

Tap the downloaded file in your browser or notification bar. If Android prompts you, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.

3

Sign In & Waddle

Tap Install (or Update), sign in with Google, grant step sensor access, and join a squad with a 6-letter code.
